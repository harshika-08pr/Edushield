🛡 About EduShield

AI-Powered Student Academic Risk Prediction System

📖 Project Overview

EduShield is an Artificial Intelligence based web application developed to identify students who may be academically at risk. Using Machine Learning techniques, the system analyses academic, psychological and lifestyle factors to predict whether a student requires early intervention.

The primary objective is to assist educators in making data-driven decisions that improve student success, reduce dropout rates and provide personalised support.

❗ Problem Statement

Educational institutions often identify struggling students only after poor academic performance has already occurred.

Manual monitoring becomes difficult when there are hundreds or thousands of students.

💡 Proposed Solution

EduShield predicts academic risk at an early stage using Machine Learning and provides actionable recommendations to support timely intervention.

🔄 Project Workflow

1️⃣

Data Collection

Student academic, psychological and lifestyle information is collected.

2️⃣

Preprocessing

Data cleaning, encoding and feature scaling are performed.

3️⃣

Model Training

Multiple ML models are trained and compared. XGBoost is selected as the best-performing model.

4️⃣

Prediction

The trained model predicts academic risk and generates useful insights.
