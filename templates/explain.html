Understand how the XGBoost model makes predictions by analysing the importance of different student-related factors.

What is Explainable AI?

Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) helps users understand how a machine learning model arrives at its prediction. Instead of acting like a "black box", the model highlights which student features contribute the most towards the final prediction.
